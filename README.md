# Consumption Mechanics

Upon consumption, meth (in-game) grants you **temporary numbness** to damage, the player cannot feel any damage but the damage accumulates. After the effects caused by meth ends, all the accumulated damage hits all at once to the player, hence causing either death or scarcity in health.

However, over consumption of substance also reduces the amount of numbness a player might feel throughout, hence making them prone to damages unlike earlier.

This causes an actual cycle of addiction in-game since if player stops consuming, he might die due to the accumultaed damage.

### Side Effects

Side effects include speed and strength, but also having possibility of getting nausea, blindness or both together.

### The Meth Bar

**The meth bar (fake breathing bar)** shows the remaining numbness effect to player, so the player can either stack or get alarmed when they're going to be hit with damage.

Upon damage it gives red-ish tint on screen and sound effects to make the overall effect more immersive.

# Production System

The creation of meth in plugin is Multi-Stage Production System.

The production system features an interconnected, multi-layered recipe progression involving several custom ingredients, intermediate materials, and workstation-specific interactions.

Unlike conventional crafting recipes, the production process is divided into multiple independent ingredient chains, which eventually converge into a unified production sequence.
